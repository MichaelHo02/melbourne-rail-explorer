# Official Melbourne photographic context

The bundled GLBs are converted from City of Melbourne's May 2020 aerial photogrammetry, not generated building textures. The [official dataset catalogue](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/city-of-melbourne-3d-textured-mesh-photomesh-2020/information/) and its [machine-readable metadata](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/city-of-melbourne-3d-textured-mesh-photomesh-2020) identify the publisher and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Credit: **City of Melbourne, 3D Textured Mesh (Photomesh) 2020. Converted from I3S to local game coordinates. CC BY 4.0.** No whole-city archive is downloaded.

The public [Southbank I3S layer](https://tiles.arcgis.com/tiles/KGdHCCUjGBpOPPac/arcgis/rest/services/Southbank_WGS84/SceneServer/layers/0?f=json) includes the river, opposite-bank skyline, west viaduct context and Southern Cross's southern roof. Its true edge follows the river beside Flinders Street even though the layer extent is rectangular. The [CBD layer](https://tiles.arcgis.com/tiles/KGdHCCUjGBpOPPac/arcgis/rest/services/CBD_WGS84/SceneServer/layers/0?f=json) supplies the actual Flinders Street heritage building and northern Southern Cross context. The selection uses disjoint hierarchy nodes, avoiding parent/child double rendering. It favours geographic coverage over the finest available LOD. The manifest retains each selected node, geometry and texture resource URL, SHA256, byte count, hierarchy level, geographic centre and actual decoded bounds.

## Runtime contract

Load the five GLBs at **identity transform**, using the same origin as the game: longitude 144.9671, latitude -37.8183. Mesh nodes already carry their local translations. Coordinates are metres: x east, y up, z south. Assets use embedded JPEG atlases and `KHR_materials_unlit` because the photographs contain baked illumination. Avoid recolouring or supplying an additional procedural facade texture. Each node is one mesh/material; load the patches independently and cull or dispose distant patches as appropriate.

The I3S service declares horizontal EPSG:4326 WGS84 and vertical EPSG:5773 EGM96. This differs from the catalogue's description of the original downloadable MGA55/AHD files. The conversion preserves **served EGM96 height**, without a guessed datum correction. `x=(longitude-144.9671)*87939`, `z=-(latitude+37.8183)*111320`, `y=served height`. Source ENU normals become east/up/minus-north. A subsequent y offset used to match the authored game's approximate track heights is an explicit *game alignment*, not a geodetic datum transformation. The current authored railway is not a surveyed vertical alignment; inspect surface intersections before enabling photographic decks next to it.

Each manifest tile has `worldBounds` and `footprintHullXZ`. The latter is the convex hull of the actual projected mesh vertices, in metres, not a survey parcel or guaranteed watertight coverage mask. It is suitable for conservative duplicate-building checks: remove a procedural footprint only when its entire footprint is inside the union of tile hulls and a corresponding photomesh volume is present. Do not blank all ground or all buildings using the group's rectangular bounds. Tiles contain roofs, streets, trees and bridge decks together; partial building cuts and missing undersides are normal. Keep station interiors, playable track, collision geometry, tunnel portals and terrain fallback. Hide authored landmark exterior meshes only after verifying their complete photographic replacement. Authoritative photographic areas have their own textures for streets and ground; overlapping flat ground may need a small depth separation or local clipping after visual review.

## Rebuild and inspect

Run `python3 scripts/prepare-photomesh.py`. The standard-library converter caches individual public resources under `/tmp/melbourne-photomesh-source`, enforces an 8 MB per-resource read guard and a 40 MB aggregate GLB guard, losslessly indexes identical complete vertices, validates vertex counts and finite values, and writes `src/data/photomesh-source.json`. The selection is fixed in the script for reproducibility; no runtime API access or key is needed.

The source provides uncompressed triangle-list geometry alongside Draco. The verified raw layout is an 8-byte count header followed by float32 position, normal and UV arrays, uint8 colour, and feature IDs/ranges. Positions are offsets from each node's WGS84/height bounding-sphere centre. The relevant [Esri I3S specification](https://github.com/Esri/i3s-spec/blob/master/format/Indexed%203d%20Scene%20Layer%20Format%20Specification.md) defines the scene hierarchy and vertex semantics. UVs preserve source `(u,v)`; a V flip was tested and rejected because it scrambled the photographed atlas. JPEG orientation and geographic geometry were checked in actual Blender renders.

Run Blender headlessly with `scripts/prepare-photomesh-review.py` to regenerate overview review images. This verifies geometry/atlas conversion, not final in-game track clearance or integration. Photogrammetry can have melted trees, occluded facades, baked vehicles and dark undersides; the capture is 2020, not a live representation. These are exterior context assets, not drivable station interiors or precise operational infrastructure.

## Integration masks

The runtime removes low photographic geometry inside the Yarra polygon and within 25 m of its bank, and within 38 m of the authored surface railway below its clearance ceiling. This removes aerial water, coarse bank folds and baked viaduct decks so authored water, banks and steel spans remain visible. These are game presentation masks, not survey edits. Source GLBs stay unchanged. The partial Flinders heritage scan is cut out to retain the complete authored facade. A procedural building is suppressed only after footprint coverage and a photographic volume ray check; masking can retain procedural sections where coverage is incomplete. Review PNGs are written to ignored `artifacts/photomesh-review/`.

The playable Southern Cross hall also retains a complete authored wave roof. Low scanned geometry in the local station hall footprint is excluded because its photographed underside forms folded, suspended surfaces from platform viewpoints; photographic patches still supply the surrounding city. This is not a surveyed replacement roof.

`src/data/station-clearance.json` retains the source footprint IDs and projected envelopes of Southern Cross conflict structures 3298 and 4244 from the building survey. They extend the existing procedural-city exclusions through the photographic volume, avoiding suspended upper sections around the approximate training alignment. These exclusions sacrifice those particular structures rather than claiming that the playable railway is accurately located through them.
