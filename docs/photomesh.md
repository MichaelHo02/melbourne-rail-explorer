# Official Melbourne photographic context

The bundled GLBs are converted from City of Melbourne's May 2020 aerial photogrammetry, not generated building textures. The [official dataset catalogue](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/explore/dataset/city-of-melbourne-3d-textured-mesh-photomesh-2020/information/) and its [machine-readable metadata](https://data.melbourne.vic.gov.au/api/explore/v2.1/catalog/datasets/city-of-melbourne-3d-textured-mesh-photomesh-2020) identify the publisher and Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence. Credit: **City of Melbourne, 3D Textured Mesh (Photomesh) 2020. Converted from I3S to local game coordinates. CC BY 4.0.** No whole-city archive is downloaded.

The public [Southbank I3S layer](https://tiles.arcgis.com/tiles/KGdHCCUjGBpOPPac/arcgis/rest/services/Southbank_WGS84/SceneServer/layers/0?f=json) includes the river, opposite-bank skyline, west viaduct context and Southern Cross's southern roof. Its true edge follows the river beside Flinders Street even though the layer extent is rectangular. The [CBD layer](https://tiles.arcgis.com/tiles/KGdHCCUjGBpOPPac/arcgis/rest/services/CBD_WGS84/SceneServer/layers/0?f=json) supplies the actual Flinders Street heritage building and northern Southern Cross context. The selection uses disjoint hierarchy nodes, avoiding parent/child double rendering. It favours geographic coverage over the finest available LOD. The manifest retains each selected node, geometry and texture resource URL, SHA256, byte count, hierarchy level, geographic centre and actual decoded bounds.

## Runtime contract

Load the five GLBs at **identity transform**, using the same origin as the game: longitude 144.9671, latitude -37.8183. Mesh nodes already carry their local translations. Coordinates are metres: x east, y up, z south. Assets use embedded JPEG atlases and `KHR_materials_unlit` because the photographs contain baked illumination. Avoid recolouring or supplying an additional procedural facade texture. Each node is one mesh/material; load the patches independently and cull or dispose distant patches as appropriate.

The I3S service declares horizontal EPSG:4326 WGS84 and vertical EPSG:5773 EGM96. This differs from the catalogue's description of the original downloadable MGA55/AHD files. The conversion preserves **served EGM96 height**, without a guessed datum correction. `x=(longitude-144.9671)*87939`, `z=-(latitude+37.8183)*111320`, `y=served height`. Source ENU normals become east/up/minus-north. A subsequent y offset used to match the authored game's approximate track heights is an explicit *game alignment*, not a geodetic datum transformation. The current authored railway is not a surveyed vertical alignment; inspect surface intersections before enabling photographic decks next to it.

Each manifest tile has `worldBounds` and `footprintHullXZ`. The latter is the convex hull of the actual projected mesh vertices, in metres, not a survey parcel or guaranteed watertight coverage mask. It is suitable for conservative duplicate-building checks: remove a procedural footprint only when its entire footprint is inside the union of tile hulls and a corresponding photomesh volume is present. Do not blank all ground or all buildings using the group's rectangular bounds. Tiles contain roofs, streets, trees and bridge decks together; partial building cuts and missing undersides are normal. Keep station interiors, playable track, collision geometry, tunnel portals and terrain fallback. Hide authored landmark exterior meshes only after verifying their complete photographic replacement. Authoritative photographic areas have their own textures for streets and ground; overlapping flat ground may need a small depth separation or local clipping after visual review.

## Rebuild and inspect

Run `python3 scripts/prepare-photomesh.py`. The standard-library converter caches individual public resources under `/tmp/melbourne-photomesh-source`, enforces an 8 MB per-resource read guard and a 40 MB aggregate GLB guard, losslessly indexes identical complete vertices, validates vertex counts and finite values, and writes `src/data/photomesh-source.json`. The selection is fixed in the script for reproducibility; no runtime API access or key is needed.

The source provides uncompressed triangle-list geometry alongside Draco. The verified raw layout is an 8-byte count header followed by float32 position, normal and UV arrays, uint8 colour, and feature IDs/ranges. Positions are offsets from each node's WGS84/height bounding-sphere centre. The relevant [Esri I3S specification](https://github.com/Esri/i3s-spec/blob/master/format/Indexed%203d%20Scene%20Layer%20Format%20Specification.md) defines the scene hierarchy and vertex semantics. UVs preserve source `(u,v)`; a V flip was tested and rejected because it scrambled the photographed atlas. JPEG orientation and geographic geometry were checked in actual Blender renders.

Run Blender headlessly with `scripts/prepare-photomesh-review.py` to regenerate overview review images. This verifies geometry/atlas conversion, not final in-game track clearance or integration. Photogrammetry can have melted trees, occluded facades, baked vehicles and dark undersides; the capture is 2020, not a live representation. These are exterior context assets, not drivable station interiors or precise operational infrastructure.

## Integration masks

The runtime removes low photographic geometry inside the Yarra polygon and within 25 m of its bank, and within 38 m of the authored surface railway below its clearance ceiling. This removes aerial water, coarse bank folds and baked viaduct decks so authored water, banks and steel spans remain visible. These are game presentation masks, not survey edits. Source GLBs stay unchanged. The partial Flinders heritage scan is cut out to retain the complete authored facade. Review PNGs are written to ignored `artifacts/photomesh-review/`.

The playable Southern Cross hall also retains a complete authored wave roof. Its existing local exclusion is 160 m across the track (−95 to +65 m), 330 m along it, centred at training route distance 1,451.372 m, world x=−1310.102, z=84.797. The exclusion now extends through the full vertical column. Previously, stopping that mask at y=45 m removed lower storeys while retaining upper photographic facade slices over the playable station. Photographic patches still supply the surrounding city. This is not a surveyed replacement roof or a claim that buildings conflicting with the approximate training alignment are absent in Melbourne.

The surrounding Southern Cross precinct also excludes photographic geometry below y=40 m within x=[−1800, −1050], z=[−450, 260]. The photographic streets, trees and occluded roof undersides in this specific area included folded surfaces starting up to roughly 19 m above the game's datum, while the authored rail is y=3 m. Their source components are often attached to tall buildings, so deleting only disconnected pieces does not solve the visible sheets. The bounded low-context cut removes those surfaces, keeps photographic silhouettes above 40 m, and conservatively retains complete surveyed procedural facades below/behind them. This is a visible presentation compromise for the approximate vertical alignment, not a ground-height correction or a claim of complete photographic coverage. The Codex browser platform review showed the glaring dark suspended sheets and distant shards removed after this change.

`src/data/station-clearance.json` retains the source footprint IDs and projected envelopes of Southern Cross conflict structures 3298 and 4244 from the building survey. They extend the existing procedural-city exclusions through the photographic volume, avoiding suspended upper sections around the approximate training alignment. These exclusions sacrifice those particular structures rather than claiming that the playable railway is accurately located through them.

### Boundary geometry and source support

`photomesh-clip.ts` subtracts each bounded convex clearance from complete triangles, instead of deciding whether to delete a triangle from its centroid. A triangle can cross a station edge even when its centre is outside, or bridge over an entire small hole with all three vertices outside. Polygon subtraction handles both cases, linearly interpolates position/normal/UV attributes on every new edge, preserves winding and discards degenerate slivers. Concave source polygons are triangulated before subtraction. Railway and bank joins use circumscribed 24-sided caps: the historic clearance radius is not narrowed, with at most 0.33 m excess for the 38 m railway radius and 0.22 m for the 25 m bank radius. Curved route spans receive a linearly varying vertical clearance ceiling.

The worker builds a spatial index, clips the 106 source tiles and produces retained steep-facade evidence. No per-triangle Three.js vectors are allocated on the main thread. Main-thread work is GLTF decoding, GPU geometry creation, and ingestion of compact facade/cut bounds; asset loading can still take several seconds. Worker failures reject the scene load, including requests queued after a failed worker startup. There is no renderer fallback or silent omission of failed assets.

Procedural-building replacement requires the existing footprint-union and roof-ray evidence **plus a retained lower facade**. Any source face touched by clipping whose bounds intersect the procedural section conservatively prevents replacement. A surviving roof or one wall can no longer erase support that clipping removed elsewhere. Explicit station-clearance conflicts are checked separately; they are intentional omissions from the approximate playable alignment, not photographic replacement claims.

The final 20 September geometry audit processed all 267,736 source triangles and produced 194,090 output triangles: Southern Cross context 22,305; viaduct context 75,867; Southbank context 31,169; Flinders context 23,386; northern Southern Cross 41,363. Runtime subtraction changes only in-memory geometry; the five bundled GLB hashes still match the original conversion manifest. `tests/photomesh-clip.test.ts` covers crossing triangles, enclosed holes, overlap subtraction, texture/normal continuity, world/local offsets, vertical ceilings, the prior Southern Cross upper-storey defect, retained-facade evidence, and the bounded low-context cut preserving higher/remote geometry. The final offline geometry-only audit took approximately 4 seconds on the development machine; that excludes texture decoding, GPU upload and shader compilation, and is not a frame-rate or full startup-time claim.

The unmodified Southern Cross context review also exposes partial tower strips at source tile edges (for example, nodes 186273/28937 contain a connected patch from about y=19.1 to 71.6 m, while other tower slivers begin above y=100 m). These are limitations of the bounded aerial capture/LOD selection, not all products of masking. Lower-facade checks retain surveyed procedural support where the scan cannot establish a complete exterior. This does not reconstruct missing photographic detail or make the 2020 capture current.

The viaduct review still shows some coarse aerial trees and jagged background context behind the train. This pass does not extend the Southern Cross low-context slab along the whole railway: doing so would discard substantially more photographed city detail and needs a separate source/asset treatment. The clipped photographic surfaces and 2023 procedural footprints can also differ at building edges because they are different capture dates and representations. Neither source is a street-level scan of the playable station.
