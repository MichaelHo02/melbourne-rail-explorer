# City Loop world pass

The horizontal route uses official DTP shape legs joined into a training circuit, with authored elevations and surveyed City of Melbourne building massing around it. Station interiors are architectural interpretations, not measured reproductions or operational signal plans.

## References

- [Metro station virtual tours](https://www.metrotrains.com.au/station-accessibility-features/virtual-station-tours/) provides official source material for further close matching of Flagstaff, Flinders Street, Melbourne Central and Parliament.
- [Transport Victoria's City Loop station overview](https://transport.vic.gov.au/help-and-support/other-public-transport-help/city-loop-and-metro-tunnel-stations) identifies the station network and underground pedestrian connections.
- [Southern Cross operator's heritage page](https://www.infranexus.com.au/southern-cross-station/our-heritage) describes its characteristic wave-shaped roof. The game now interprets that form with a broad undulating canopy and repeated steel ribs.

## Implemented

- Platforms, platform edge strips, walls, ceilings, fittings and passengers follow the same route curve as the train. The lateral basis is normalized on grades.
- The first platform extends the route endpoint tangent instead of collapsing all geometry behind route origin onto one point.
- Underground shell segment boundaries exactly coincide with station chambers. Arch-shaped end walls close the difference between the station chamber and smaller running tunnel.
- Shallow approach trenches cut openings in the city ground, with concrete retaining walls. The tunnel roof begins only below ground level.
- Flinders Street pitched iron canopies and Southern Cross waveform canopy differentiate the two surface stops.
- Underground stations have repeated wall/ceiling structure, exit signs and station color bands. Colors and fixtures are illustrative.
- Passengers use material-batched geometry, varied clothes, articulated limbs, hair and backpacks. These remain simple background figures.
- Nearby stations only are drawn; repeated fittings merge by material. HDRI outdoor background is preserved, and environment lighting fades underground.

## Remaining fidelity limits

The prototype does not reproduce the real multi-track station layouts, escalator shafts, complete overhead wiring, real signal aspects, every station frontage or street traffic. The building survey provides geographic massing rather than photographic facades. Train physics and this environment must not be used for operational training.

## Reference-based station revision

The second pass replaces the earlier box-and-dome landmark with `public/models/stations-flinders.glb`, generated by `scripts/blender-stations.py` and retained as editable `assets/source/stations/flinders-street.blend`. The model uses eight material batches. It includes the long redbrick and ochre rendered facade, repeated arched window surrounds and muntins, clock tower, main copper dome, cupolas, entrance clock bank, chimneys and a scalloped platform verandah. The facade was checked against the user-supplied HCMT platform reference and the [Victorian Heritage Database description](https://vhd.heritage.vic.gov.au/search/nattrust_result_detail/64960). [The Victorian government's restoration account](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/first-glimpse-historic-flinders-street-station-facade) provides further context for the restored facade. The building is hand-authored from reference, not a photogrammetric scan.

The matching surveyed massing must be excluded to prevent two buildings occupying the same location. The game anchors the model to the main eastern dome at approximately 144.96714, -37.81793, with the long wing running west. The playable Flinders platform now lies on the northern, building-facing side of the running line. Its seven-car stop marker matches the current train. A broad ballast apron, ten scenic running roads and five additional island platforms establish the surrounding railway precinct. These roads illustrate the station context and do not claim an exact surveyed track arrangement. Southern Cross now has a 98 metre wide waveform roof, skylight strips, branching steel columns and adjacent scenic platforms.

`assets/source/stations/flinders-review.png` is a Blender-rendered geometry review. An intermediate browser inspection confirmed landmark loading with no page errors, but also exposed survey-block overlap and the former platform side; those were subsequently corrected in the shared implementation. Final integrated verification belongs in the Codex browser as requested by the user. The production build passed with the official DTP horizontal route replacement. Ground heights, platform structures and tunnel depths remain authored.

## Southern Cross survey collision audit

Route sampling against the original polygons identified source `objectid` 3295 and 3296 (structure 812676) as the broad station base/roof volumes covering the playable line at approximately 1408–1621 metres. These two exact sections are excluded in favour of the authored open railway hall; neighbouring tower sections of the same source structure remain. Section 4244 is classified `Bridge` by the source, but its ground-relative extrusion fills the underpass at 1624–1636 metres, so that section is also excluded. A separate four-part commercial building (28201–28204) intersects the coarse public service alignment at 1342–1372 metres. Its exclusion provides game clearance and is explicitly not a claim that the real line goes through that building.

The reproducible preparation script records these exact IDs in the generated dataset metadata. This removes seven sections: 8,548 before this audit, 8,541 after. Production build passed after regeneration. Final Southern Cross visual verification is performed in the root task's Codex browser.

A second Southern Cross audit samples the actual platform corridor at lateral offsets +2, +4, +6 and +8 metres over station distance −168 to +38 metres. It finds source sections 3298/3299 occupying the platform from approximately 1404–1552 metres, with 3297/3302 continuing into the canopy. Their connected upper caps 3300/3301/3303 are removed with that same stack to avoid floating geometry. These are seven further exact-ID removals, not a blanket station-area mask. Final retained count is **8,534 building sections**. The other northern sections of source structure 812676 and unrelated surrounding towers remain. Production build passes after regeneration.
